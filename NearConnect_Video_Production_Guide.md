# NearConnect App - Video Production Guide

## Production Overview

### Video Specifications
- **Duration**: 60-90 seconds
- **Aspect Ratios**: 
  - 16:9 (YouTube, website)
  - 9:16 (Instagram Stories, TikTok)
  - 1:1 (Instagram feed)
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (Full HD) minimum
- **Frame Rate**: 30fps
- **Audio**: Stereo, 48kHz, 16-bit
- **Format**: MP4 (H.264 codec)

---

## Pre-Production Checklist

### 1. Script & Storyboard
- ✅ Final script approved
- ✅ Storyboard completed
- ✅ Shot list prepared
- ✅ Location scouting done

### 2. Casting & Talent
- **Main Characters**: 4-6 diverse young adults (ages 20-35)
- **Extras**: 10-15 people for group scenes
- **Requirements**: 
  - Comfortable on camera
  - Authentic, natural expressions
  - Diverse representation
  - Professional appearance

### 3. Locations
- **Coffee Shop**: Modern, well-lit café
- **Shopping Mall**: Clean, contemporary retail space
- **Gym/Fitness Center**: Modern equipment, good lighting
- **Outdoor Space**: Park or plaza for meeting scenes
- **Office/Co-working Space**: For task collaboration scene

### 4. Equipment List
- **Camera**: 4K capable (Sony A7S III, Canon R5, or similar)
- **Lenses**: 24-70mm, 50mm, 85mm primes
- **Audio**: Wireless lavalier mics, boom mic
- **Lighting**: LED panels, reflectors, diffusers
- **Stabilization**: Gimbal, tripod, slider
- **Post-Production**: High-end editing workstation

---

## Production Schedule

### Day 1: App Interface & Animation Shots
- **Duration**: 4-6 hours
- **Shots**: All app interface demonstrations
- **Setup**: Green screen, controlled lighting
- **Talent**: Not required

### Day 2: Real-World Scenarios
- **Duration**: 6-8 hours
- **Shots**: Coffee shop, shopping, gym, meeting scenes
- **Setup**: Natural lighting with fill lights
- **Talent**: All main characters

### Day 3: Pickup Shots & B-Roll
- **Duration**: 3-4 hours
- **Shots**: Additional coverage, establishing shots
- **Setup**: Various locations
- **Talent**: As needed

## Technical Implementation

### App Interface Mockups
Create high-fidelity mockups of:
1. **Map Interface**: 
   - User location (red pin)
   - Nearby users (colored dots)
   - Activity markers
   - Search bar

2. **Matching Interface**:
   - Profile cards
   - Swipe gestures
   - Compatibility scores
   - Interest tags

3. **Chat Interface**:
   - Message bubbles
   - Voice message indicators
   - Activity invitations
   - Location sharing

4. **Settings Interface**:
   - Privacy controls
   - Location sharing toggles
   - Anonymous mode
   - Security badges

### Animation Requirements
- **Logo Animation**: 3D or 2D animation software (After Effects, Blender)
- **App Transitions**: Smooth, iOS/Android-style animations
- **Particle Effects**: Subtle, professional particles
- **Text Animations**: Typewriter effect, fade-ins

### Color Grading
- **Primary Colors**: 
  - Brand Blue: #3182ce
  - Brand Green: #38a169
  - Neutral Gray: #4a5568
- **Mood**: Bright, optimistic, professional
- **Contrast**: High contrast for mobile viewing

## Post-Production Workflow

### 1. Editing (Week 1)
- **Software**: Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, or DaVinci Resolve
- **Timeline**: 90-second master cut
- **Rough Cut**: 2-3 days
- **Fine Cut**: 2-3 days
- **Client Review**: 1 day

### 2. Motion Graphics (Week 2)
- **Software**: After Effects
- **Elements**: 
  - Logo animations
  - App interface mockups
  - Text overlays
  - Transition effects
- **Delivery**: 4K source files

### 3. Audio Production (Week 2)
- **Voiceover**: Professional voice actor
- **Music**: Licensed track or custom composition
- **Sound Effects**: App sounds, ambient audio
- **Software**: Pro Tools, Logic Pro

### 4. Final Delivery (Week 3)
- **Formats**: 
  - 16:9 (1920x1080) - YouTube, website
  - 9:16 (1080x1920) - Instagram Stories, TikTok
  - 1:1 (1080x1080) - Instagram feed
- **Compression**: Optimized for each platform
- **Quality Check**: Final review and approval

## Risk Mitigation

### Technical Risks
- **Backup**: Multiple camera angles, redundant recording
- **Quality**: Test all equipment before production
- **Format**: Shoot in highest quality available

### Creative Risks
- **Feedback**: Regular client check-ins during production
- **Flexibility**: Allow for creative adjustments
- **Testing**: Preview versions for stakeholder approval

### Timeline Risks
- **Buffer**: 20% time buffer in schedule
- **Priority**: Identify critical vs. nice-to-have elements
- **Contingency**: Backup plans for weather, location issues
